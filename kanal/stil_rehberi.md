# Rebel Minds: Cosmos — Style Guide

## Voice
Curious, slightly rebellious, evidence-driven. We question the consensus but never overclaim. We make the viewer feel smart, not lectured. No hype words ("scientists are shocked", "this changes everything", "proves").

## Script
- Language: English. Pace: ~140 words per minute. Default length: 10 minutes.
- First 15 seconds: a hook built on genuine tension or a question — no false promises.
- Short-to-medium sentences written for text-to-speech; no parentheses; numbers written the way they should be spoken.
- Cite every factual sentence with [S1], [S2]… mapped to a `## Sources` list.
- Put `[EVIDENCE: short label]` on its own line right before a NEW piece of evidence is introduced (drives the evidence tracker).
- Open curiosity loops and pay them off. Reflective close, one natural call to action.

## Visuals
- Colourful, constantly moving cosmic imagery. Even general statements get cosmic cutaways (star clusters, planets, discs, nebulae) — never a static or empty frame.
- Never the same visual style twice in a row; never the same image twice in an episode.
- Prefer real imagery (NASA/ESA/ESO) when the narration refers to a real mission, object or instrument.
- Every scene has motion: slow push-in, pull-out, pan, parallax, rotation.

## On-screen text (must be legible on a phone)
- Max 6 words per overlay, large bold sans-serif (min 64 px height on a 1080p frame), inside 10% safe margins, high contrast (soft dark scrim when needed).
- Few overlays rather than many — only where they help understanding or land a key number.

## Evidence tracker
A recurring graphic that appears ONLY when a new piece of evidence is introduced (count + short label), then disappears. Never permanent clutter.

## Palette & type
- Deep space navy `#0B1026`, cosmic violet `#6C3BFF`, solar amber `#FFB020`, ice white `#F2F5FF`
- Montserrat ExtraBold (headings/overlays), Inter (small labels)

## Thumbnail
Max 4 words, one focal subject, readable at phone size, palette colours.
